What is this?

This is a demo for each currently active and in beta application. Each demo comes pre-loaded with 90 days of realistic sample data so you can explore insights, patterns, and every feature right away, with no sign-up or setup required.

You have 3 days to explore from whenever you first open a demo. After that, if you'd like to stay in the loop and know when the app is released publicly, leave your email address with us in the Google Form here and we'll send you a link to the app as soon as it's completed.

The sample data and the data that you input into each app lives only on your device and disappears when you clear your browser data. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
